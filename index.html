Jonathan Graham

Co-founder & CTO at StudyStash

Jonathan Graham is the CTO and co-founder of StudyStash, an AI-powered adaptive learning platform acquired by Kortext in 2026. Used by tens of thousands of students across three continents, including LSU, Loughborough University, Cal State (CSU) and the California Community Colleges system.

He studied Computer Science at the University of Birmingham, where StudyStash began.
